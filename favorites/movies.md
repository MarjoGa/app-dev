# My Favorite Movies

Here are my five favorite movies:

1. **Avengers: Endgame**
   - A superhero movie about the Avengers trying to save the universe.

2. **Spider-Man: No Way Home**
   - A movie about Spider-Man facing challenges with other versions of himself.

3. **Fast and Furious 7**
   - An action movie about friendship, family, and exciting car races.

4. **Harry Potter**
   - A fantasy movie about a young wizard and his adventures.

5. **The Conjuring**
   - A horror movie about a family experiencing supernatural events.
